Handover (wisselmoment) – step-by-step workflow

For Kretoss · 16 November 2026 · version 2

The handover process starts in the agenda with the fixed handover moments. The checklist (Mee met de wissel) is one step in that process. It fills itself from three sources and is checked by the receiving parent after the handover. Sketches of every screen are at the end of this document, translated into English and all in the final style (A6) with the new child colours: build them like this. Colours, cards and fonts are in Stijlgids v1.2.

Two terms used below

  • Handing-over parent: the parent the children are with before the handover. This parent packs.
  • Receiving parent: the parent the children go to.

Your questions, short answers

  • Complete process? Steps 1 to 11 below, from the agenda to the confirmation after the handover.
  • What can parents add? An item, chosen from a fixed list of options or typed by the parent, for one or more children.
  • Both parents view and update? Both parents see the list and can add items. Who may tick items as packed is still an open question on our side (see the end).
  • History? Handover moments stay visible in the agenda forever. Packing lists are deleted one month after the handover.

Setup, once

Step 1 – Fixed handover moments in the agenda (sketch 1)

  1. A parent enters the fixed schedule from the parenting plan: which days the children are with whom.
  2. Per handover: day, time, Location (free text field, for example "School" or "Thuis"), Brengt / Haalt (brings or picks up) and which parent. Example: Friday 14:30, School, Joris picks up.
  3. The other parent confirms with Klopt / Klopt niet. Klopt niet: the other parent writes what is different, the first parent adjusts, and the other parent confirms again.
  4. After confirmation, all handover moments show in the agenda for both parents. They stay visible forever.

Step 2 – Fixed items per child (sketch 2)

  1. Per child, parents choose the items that go along at every handover: from the option list or typed themselves.
  2. Both parents can change this list. The other parent gets a notification of every change.

Step 3 – Items linked to an appointment (sketch 3)

  1. When a parent creates an appointment (for example swimming lesson, Saturday 10:00, every week, for Noah), there is a field Neem mee (take along).
  2. The parent chooses items from the option list or types them. The app never pre-fills or suggests items here. Empty by default.
  3. For a recurring appointment this is done once.

Around every handover

Step 4 – Moving a handover (only when needed) (sketch 4, button "Wissel verschuiven")

  1. A parent proposes to move the handover one or more days earlier or later.
  2. The other parent agrees → the handover moves in the agenda. The checklist moves with it and is rebuilt for the new dates (step 5).
  3. The other parent says no → the fixed handover moment stays.

Step 5 – The checklist builds itself, 48 hours before the handover (sketches 4 and 7)

  1. 48 hours before the handover, the app creates the checklist and sends the handing-over parent a notification: "De lijst voor vrijdag staat klaar."
  2. The list contains automatically:
    1. the fixed items of the children (step 2)
    2. the items of appointments that fall between this handover and the next one, while the children are with the receiving parent (step 3)
    3. items that did not arrive at the previous handover (step 10)
  3. Each item shows where it comes from: "Vast", the appointment ("Zwemles za 10:00"), "Verzoek van [naam]" or "Niet meegekomen vorige keer".
  4. Where to find it: on Home as a line under Nog te regelen ("Paklijst voor vrijdag · 0 van 5"), by tapping the handover dot in the lint of the Vandaag card, and in the agenda by tapping the handover moment (sketch 4). There is no separate packing-list card under the lint.

Step 6 – Request from the receiving parent

  1. The receiving parent can add an item to the list, for example "Winterjas". It shows as "Verzoek van [naam]".
  2. The handing-over parent gets a notification.

Step 7 – Reminder the evening before (sketch 7)

  1. The evening before the handover, the handing-over parent gets: "Morgen wissel. Klopt de lijst?" with the packed count.
  2. Never during the parent's quiet hours (Rustmodus).

Step 8 – Packing (sketches 4, 5 and 6)

  1. Items are ticked as packed. Home and the agenda show "2 van 5 ingepakt".
  2. Iets toevoegen opens the option list (sketch 5). Tap one or more options, then Toevoegen (n).
  3. Zelf iets toevoegen lets the parent type an item (sketch 6). A typed item is saved as an extra option for next time.

Step 9 – The handover moment (sketch 7)

On Home the handover is part of the lint in the Vandaag card (bij jou → wissel → bij de ander), with day and time. The packing list is a line under Nog te regelen with the packed count. Both open the checklist with day, time, location and who brings or picks up.

After the handover

Step 10 – The receiving parent confirms (sketch 8)

  1. One hour after the handover time, the receiving parent gets: "Is alles meegekomen?"
  2. The list shows every item with Ontvangen or Niet meegekomen; the parent taps an item to change it, then Bevestigen.
  3. The handing-over parent gets a notification with the result.
  4. Items marked "Niet meegekomen" go automatically onto the list of the next handover.

Step 11 – Closing

  1. The handover moment stays in the agenda forever. Tapping a past handover shows its checklist.
  2. The checklist is deleted one month after the handover. After that, the past handover shows without a list.

Look and feel in the agenda

  • Days the children are with this parent: soft green fill with a 2 px green border. Days without children: soft diagonal stripes. Never black or dark blocks.
  • The handover shows as a small label in the day: ⇄ 14:30. The direction is clear from the border and stripes, so no extra text.
  • Appointments: a small dot in the child's colour with the name of the appointment in small letters, under each other.
  • Child colours come from the Kinderen page (6 earthy colours, see Stijlgids v1.2).
  • Tapping a day opens the day view; tapping the handover opens the checklist (sketch 4).

Related: Lastige momenten

These features are described in Co-and-Lastige-momenten-for-Kretoss.pdf; they touch the handover:

  • Het komt eraan: only if the parent turned on "Help me bij elke wissel". The evening before the handover, a line under Nog te regelen with the parent's own sentence, the packing list and something for themselves.
  • Hoe ging de wissel?: one hour after the handover, a private question for a parent who turned this on. This is separate from "Is alles meegekomen?" (step 10), which always goes to the receiving parent.
  • Lastig bij de wissel?: a small link on the checklist screen that opens the topic Overdracht in Lastige momenten.

Option list (Iets toevoegen)

Zwemspullen · Gymtas · Medicijnen · Voetbalspullen · Sportkleding · Schooltas · Knuffel · Pyjama · Jas · Oplader · Tablet · Huiswerk — plus every item a parent typed earlier. Please keep this list in a config file we can edit.

Data per checklist item

item_id, handover_id, name, child_ids[], source (vast / afspraak / verzoek / toegevoegd / niet_meegekomen), source_ref (appointment id or parent id), packed, packed_by, packed_at, received (true / false), deleted_at (handover + 1 month).

Not part of the handover

Nog nodig (things to buy) is a separate list under Kosten. It has no link with the handover or the checklist.

Still open on our side

  • Who may tick items as packed: only the handing-over parent, or both parents?
  • The exact time of the evening reminder (step 7).
