## What We Built Today (In Simple Terms)

Today we focused on three major improvements: **inviting multiple co-parents**, **individual weekly schedules per child**, and **profile photos (avatars) for both parents and children**.

---

### 1. Inviting Multiple Co-Parents
- **Support for Blended Families**: Parents can now invite more than one co-parent to the app. This is perfect for families where different children have different co-parents.
- **Choose Which Child to Share**: When inviting a co-parent, you choose exactly which child (or children) to share. Unselected children stay completely private to you.
- **Clear Status Banners on the Children Page**:
  - Each connected co-parent gets their own card showing their name, email, and which children they are linked to.
  - Children without a shared co-parent clearly display as **"Private · Solo"**, with a quick button to invite a co-parent just for that child.

---

### 2. Individual Weekly Schedules per Child
- **Separate Weekly Schedule for Each Child**: Instead of forcing all children into one schedule, each child can now have their own independent care routine (e.g. week-on/week-off, custom days, or weekend stays).
- **Privacy for Co-Parents**: A connected co-parent will only see the weekly schedule for the child they share with you. They cannot see schedules for other children in your family.

---

### 3. Profile Photos (Avatars) for Both Parents & Children
- **Photos for Parents**:
  - Parents can now upload a profile picture from their Account Settings.
  - The photo appears neatly in the top menu bar next to your name.
- **Photos for Children**:
  - You can now upload a photo for each child when adding or editing their profile.
  - The child's photo displays on their profile card, the Children overview page, and the care schedule.
  - If no photo is uploaded, it automatically shows a neat color badge with the child's first initial.

---

- Added the official **Ouderschap & Co.** logo and website icon (favicon) to the app.
- Ensured all new screens and buttons work smoothly in both **Dutch** and **English**.
